Privacy Policies

IA Engineering · offline mobile games

Publisher: IA Engineering · Privacy contact: info@iaengineering.net · Effective date: 23 September 2026

What every game on this page has in common

  • No account. None of the games ask you to sign in or create a profile.
  • No personal-data collection. The publisher does not collect personal information from these games. They do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, health information, or an account. Lantern Table lets players enter short clues on the shared device; these clues are kept only in the current app session and are never transmitted.
  • No network connection. The Android apps request no Internet permission. Nothing you do in a game is transmitted to the publisher or to any third party.
  • No ads, analytics, or purchases. There is no advertising, analytics SDK, in-app purchasing, online matchmaking, online chat, or online service of any kind.
  • Local storage only. Each game keeps its progress in local app storage on your device. You can erase it with the in-game reset control, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game. The release builds disable Android auto-backup for this data.
  • No third-party SDKs. The games contain no third-party advertising, analytics, or sign-in components.
  • Children. None of the games knowingly collect information from children or from any other player, and none offer online chat or online user-generated content sharing. Lantern Table player labels and clues stay in the current on-device session.

This page describes the data practices in the privacy notice bundled inside each game, effective from 23 September 2026. The public wording was clarified on 4 October 2026 to explain Lantern Table's local clue entry; the data practices have not changed. If a future edition changes these data practices, this page and the in-app notice will be updated before that edition is released.

01 · Railwright Relay

Offline route-planning puzzle

Stored on your device: campaign progress, completed Daily Freight routes, sound and reduced-motion preferences, and whether the first-run tip was dismissed. Nothing is sent to the publisher or another company.

The optional browser preview keeps the same progress in the browser's local site storage, which the browser's own privacy controls can clear.

02 · Last Call Archive

Offline mystery archive game

Stored on your device: case progress, earned stars, and settings. The stories are fictional, non-graphic mysteries; the app requests no sensitive device permissions, and optional sound effects are generated locally.

03 · Beat Foundry

Offline rhythm and music game

Stored on your device: song scores, calibration, settings, and daily play records. All music and hit sounds are generated locally from original synthesis patterns.

04 · Pocket Hex

Offline run-based puzzle

Stored on your device: run progress, best room, unlocks, sound preference, and accessibility preference. Short sound tones are generated locally.

05 · Touchline Tactics

Offline sports management

Stored on your device: season table, current fixture, training choices, sound preference, and accessibility preference. Erase them with Reset Season in Settings or by clearing the app's storage. The game uses fictional teams and players and has no chat or user-generated content.

06 · Drift Circuit

Offline arcade racing

Stored on your device: personal best times, local ghost runs, selected circuit, car colour, sound preference, and reduced-motion preference. Erase them by clearing the app's storage in Android settings. The game contains fictional circuits and vehicles.

07 · Reefkeeper

Offline ecology sandbox

Stored on your device: habitat progress, tile states, report scores, selected habitat, sound preference, and reduced-motion preference. Erase them with Reset field notebook in Settings or by clearing the app's storage.

Reefkeeper uses fictional habitats and a simplified learning model. It is not a scientific forecasting or field-management tool.

08 · Lantern Table

Local pass-and-play social deduction

Stored on your device: table settings and aggregate record counts. During a round, generated player labels (such as Player 1), roles, player-entered clues, votes, and the secret word exist only in the current app session and disappear when the table ends. Erase the record with Reset table record in Settings.

09 · Runway Remix

Offline styling and scoring game

Stored on your device: selected brief, mannequin, look count, best score, sound preference, and reduced-motion preference. Erase them with Reset studio record in Settings. The game uses fictional garments and a transparent local rubric and makes no real-brand or popularity claims.

10 · Starfall Salvage

Offline physics arcade

Stored on your device: selected flight plan, tug, recovery count, best score, flight count, sound preference, and reduced-motion preference. Erase them with Reset flight record in Settings. The game features fictional cargo and hazards, contains no weapons, and makes no real-world navigation claim.
